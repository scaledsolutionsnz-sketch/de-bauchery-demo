Homekill, game & recreational catch

Your beast, your cuts, back in your freezer.

Most people ring us because they have an animal ready and no idea what happens next. Here is the whole thing, start to finish, so you know what to expect before you pick up the phone.

How it runs

Four steps, no surprises.

1

Ring the shop

Tell us what you have, roughly what it weighs and when it will be ready. We put you in the book so the chiller space is there when your animal arrives.

2

Fill in the cut sheet

The cut sheet is where you decide everything: steak thickness, roasts, boned and rolled, mince, sausages, vac packing. It has to be completed and signed before we start cutting.

3

We break it down

The carcass is broken down by hand on the block, then wrapped and labelled so you can tell a rump from a blade in six months' time without opening the bag.

4

Collect from Tikipunga

We ring when it is ready and you collect from 171 Kiripaka Road. Bring chilly bins or an empty boot, because a half beast takes up more room than people expect.

Wild game

Deer, pig, goat and your recreational catch.

If you have been out over the weekend, bring it in cool, clean and as soon as you can. The better the condition it turns up in, the better it comes back out. We will talk you through what is worth keeping whole, what is better as mince, and what should go into salami or sausage.

  • Ring first. A quick call means chiller space is ready when you turn up.
  • Keep it cool and clean. Skinned, hung and out of the sun beats a hot ute tray every time.
  • Smallgoods on the same order. Salami, sausage and pastrami all made in the shop.
