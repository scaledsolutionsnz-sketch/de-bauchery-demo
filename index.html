What comes off the block

Six things the shop is set up to do.

A working butchery, not a supermarket chiller. Whether it is your own beast, your deer from the weekend, or a tray of chops for Tuesday, it gets handled by hand on Kiripaka Road.

Homekill

Homekill cut & wrap

Your beast comes in, you fill out a cut sheet, and it goes back out the door as the cuts you actually eat. Boned and rolled, vac packed, mince, sausages, whatever you tick. Everything tagged from drop-off to pick-up.

Wild game

Wild game & recreational catch

Deer, pig, goat and your own recreational catch, processed properly. Bring it in cool and clean and we will turn it into steaks, mince, salami and sausage rather than a freezer full of guesswork.

Made in store

House-made smallgoods

Sausages, bacon, salami, pastrami, ham and black pudding, all made here rather than bought in. Plain snarlers for the kids, flavoured ones for everyone else, and enough salami to be a problem.

Retail

The retail counter

Walk in and buy dinner. Steak, mince, chops, roasts, bacon and smallgoods over the counter, cut fresh rather than pre-packed a week ago in another city. Coffee on while you wait.

Ask us

Cut to order

Thick cut or thin, French trimmed racks, a whole scotch fillet for the smoker, party sausages for the weekend. Give us a ring with what you are cooking and we will have it ready to collect.

Farm orders

Farm order collection

We are the Whangārei processing partner for Out the Farm Gate, and the Northland collection point for growers like Wild Meadows and Backyard Beef. Order from the farmer, pick it up from us in Tikipunga.

The shop

A butchery, run by a butcher.

De Bauchery sits on Kiripaka Road in Tikipunga, trading as Tikipunga Fresh Foods. It is a working shop: chiller, block, mincer, smoker and a counter out the front. The emphasis has always been on the work rather than the fit-out.

Nick and the team handle retail butchery, homekill and recreational catch for households and lifestyle blocks right across the Whangārei area, and make the smallgoods in house rather than buying them in. If you want to know what is in your sausages, you can ask the person who made them.

  • Locally owned and on the tools. Same faces behind the block every week.
  • Your cut sheet, your cuts. Nothing gets decided for you and everything is labelled.
  • Smallgoods made on site. Sausages, bacon, salami, pastrami, ham and black pudding.
